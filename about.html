About

Coordination work grounded in communication.

I move between the practical side of getting things done — logistics, budgets, stakeholders — and the communications side of explaining why it matters, which is what drew me from a media and literature background into project and event coordination.

Background

My path started in literature and cultural studies, moved through a Master of Development Studies, and has landed in Communication and Media Studies at the University of Calgary — a combination that's given me a habit of asking who an audience is before deciding how to reach them.

In practice, that's shown up as coordinating national workshops for government stakeholders and media personnel, liaising monthly with an international donor on behalf of a Dhaka-based nonprofit, and building a content and marketing program from the ground up. It's also shown up in the classroom, supporting a 240-student communication course and running weekly tutorials of my own.

I'm currently based in Calgary, continuing my graduate studies while looking for coordination and communications work with organizations whose mission I can get behind — like XPRIZE Canada Hub's work connecting innovators across climate, energy, health, and deep technology.

2023 – 2024 · PROGGA — Knowledge for Progress

Donor liaison & national workshops for Vitamin A policy

As Project Coordinator, I was the primary point of contact between PROGGA and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation — organizing monthly progress meetings and writing up post-event reports for the donor group.

On the ground, that meant planning and running the logistics for national workshops in support of government Vitamin A fortification policy, and facilitating sessions for government officials and media personnel, including preparing media briefs and presenter slides for each one.

Donor relations Event logistics Workshop facilitation
2023 · WHO Bangladesh

Health-Promoting Schools national guidelines

I composed the first draft of national guidelines on Health-Promoting Schools for the World Health Organization's Bangladesh office, built from a round of multi-stakeholder consultation across the sector.

Policy writing Stakeholder consultation
2024 – 2025 · 4 Dimension

Amazon Affiliate marketing program, from zero

As Project Manager, I piloted two Amazon Affiliate marketing projects from scratch — building organic traffic from zero to 100,000 visits in 16 months, and reaching US$36,208 in revenue in 2025. Alongside that, I ran a content marketing campaign aimed at digital visibility, and kept investors, management, and staff aligned throughout.

SEO Content marketing Stakeholder communication
